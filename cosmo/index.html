Cosmo · Cognitive Operating System — Memory & Orchestration
The idea

The intelligence already exists. Cosmo is the body and the memory around it.

Frontier AI can already think, plan, and reason. What it lacks is a persistent memory of your life and the ability to act inside it. Cosmo is that missing layer: a structured memory of everything you're working toward, a set of skills that act on it, and a conductor that interleaves every goal into a single coherent day.

System architecture

One store. One conductor. Every part a window into the same mind.

Cosmo's features aren't separate apps bolted together — they're operations on one shared memory, orchestrated by one engine. Here's how it all connects.

A day with Cosmo

It doesn't wait to be opened. It runs the day around you.

The day is a closed loop — the evening sets tomorrow, which feeds the morning. You're always in conversation; the screen just shows what the conversation is looking at.

06:30
Morning brief
Wakes you the way last night decided. Speaks the day — goals, schedule, a brief per pillar, weather, what's on the radar.
07:00
The sit-down
"Let's go through today." You talk freely; it reshuffles the schedule in real time and flags conflicts.
09:30
Deep work
Protects the block. Hand it real work — "research this, draft that" — and it reports back when done.
14:00
Midday reset
A quick pulse — what's done, what's left, is the rest of the day still realistic? Re-plans on the fly.
21:00
Wind-down
Walks the day back with you, captures your reflection, and quietly promotes what matters into long-term memory.
21:15
Tomorrow
Lays out tomorrow, takes your changes, locks it in — and sets how hard to wake you. The loop closes.
What makes it different

Built on principles, not features.

Yours, and local

Your life stays on your machine. No cloud copy of the things that matter most. Private by architecture, not by promise.

Never a black box

Cosmo shows its work — how it decided, why, and where a fact came from. You stay in control of every judgment.

You confirm, it acts

Nothing touches your life without your word. It proposes; you decide. Trust is earned, then widened.

The conductor

Its real job isn't any single feature — it's holding every goal at once and weaving them into one day you can actually live.

A plan, not a nag

Goals are thought through fully in advance, so day-to-day you execute pre-decided actions instead of deciding on the spot.

Grows from use

New skills and mentors are added as text, not code — the system gets smarter the more you live in it, not more cluttered.
