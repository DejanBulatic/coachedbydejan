Questions

Things you might
be wondering

Do I need any experience to start?
Not at all. Most of my clients are beginners or returning after a long break. The programme is built around where you are right now.
What equipment do I need?
Your programme is built around what you have access to — gym, home, or outdoors. You tell me, I adapt.
How does the app work?
After you sign up, you receive a private app on your phone. Your workouts, nutrition plan, check-ins, and our communication all live there.
Can I do this with an injury or health condition?
Yes. The intake process includes a health screening. I work around limitations, not against them.
What happens after 12 weeks?
We review your results and discuss what comes next. Many clients continue. No pressure — just an honest conversation.
Is coaching available in my language?
Coaching is available in English, Swedish, and Serbian. The intake form and app are in all three languages.
