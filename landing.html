Questions

Things you might
be wondering

Do I need any experience to start?
Not at all. Most of my clients are beginners or returning after a long break. The programme is built around where you are right now — not where you think you should be.
What equipment do I need?
Your programme is built around what you have access to — gym, home, or outdoors. You tell me, I adapt.
How does the app work?
After you sign up, you receive a link and login to a private app on your phone. Your workouts, nutrition plan, check-in form, and our communication all live there. I walk you through it on your onboarding call.
Can I do this if I have an injury or health condition?
Yes. The intake process includes a health screening. I work around limitations, not against them. If something requires medical clearance, I'll let you know.
What happens after 12 weeks?
We review your results together and discuss what comes next. Many clients continue with a renewed programme. There's no pressure — just an honest conversation about where you want to go.
Is coaching available in my language?
Coaching is available in English, Swedish, and Serbian. The intake form and app are available in all three languages.
